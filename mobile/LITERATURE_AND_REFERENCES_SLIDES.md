### Speaker explanation for this slide

The literature survey shows that personalized mobile health applications can support physical activity, weight management and healthy lifestyle behaviour. The research also shows that personalization alone is not enough. User trust, privacy, transparency, motivation and cultural relevance are important. These findings influenced the design of this project: the application combines profile-based planning, habit tracking, explanations, offline access and security considerations.

### Speaker explanation for this slide

The proposed application connects health assessment, diet, recipes, substitutions, grocery planning and habit tracking in one mobile workflow.
